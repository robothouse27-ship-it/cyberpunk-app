This scenario is designed to play using the fence

 GREEN and adds their leader’s

places their team within RED 
 RED of that board quarter’s

 RED of the

models in Reserve.

with the Shocker, the players take turns placing a 
single section of fence anywhere on the battlespace.

so they touch) increases their strength (see Electric 
Fences), and the player who placed each section of 
fence is not tracked (or important).
The Shocker begins with control.
ELECTRIC FENCES
The fences are electric! Models may take the De-
Activate action.
De-Activate actions and Reflexes check made to 
parkour/climb/traverse any fence section are 
Dangerous. 
In addition, if three or more fence sections are 
connected together (touching in an unbroken chain) 
then models suffer 2 wounds from the Dangerous 
effect instead of 1. 
If five or more fence sections are connected together, 
then the model is Take Out by the Dangerous effect, 
intead of suffering wounds.
THE COOL
Those sparks arc! Whenever a model triggers the 
Dangerous effect of an Electric Fence, either player 
may spend a Luck token to force another model within 
YELLOW 
YELLOW to roll Reflexes vs the Obstacle die or suffer 
the same effect. 
THE WIN
A player wins immediately when they take a fifth Fence 
section via the De-Activate action. If every model dies 
before this can be done, the Tazer player wins.
CAMPAIGN FALLOUT
The winner of this game chooses a model to promote.
DE-ACTIVATE
[Dangerous] Remove a single Fence section within Reach and set it aside as your score. The 
Dangerous damage from this action is increased based on the number of connected fences 
(see Electric Fences).
